# Life Assist — Support

Life Assist is one quiet place to run your life: a main goal, honest
money and time numbers, habits that check themselves, and notes that
link like a mind map. Local-first — no account, no cloud.

## Common questions

**Where is my data?**
On your device, in a local database. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
Settings → Export backup gives you a complete JSON copy; Settings →
Notes vault exports your notes as Markdown files you can open in any
editor (including Obsidian).

**How do I move to a new phone?**
Export a backup on the old phone (Settings → Export backup → save the
file), install Life Assist on the new one, then Settings → Import
backup.

**Habits aren't checking themselves.**
Settings → Apple Health → allow access, then edit a habit and map it
to a metric (steps, sleep, mindfulness, workouts). Apple hides
whether access was granted — if nothing appears, re-check the Health
app → Sharing → Apps → Life Assist. Your own manual check always wins
over the sensor.

**Siri doesn't understand "log an expense in Life Assist."**
Open the app once after installing (phrases register on first
launch), then try again. Captures made while the app is closed appear
the next time you open it.

**Widgets say "Open Life Assist for a fresh look."**
Open the app once — widgets refresh from the last time the app ran.

**A reminder didn't fire.**
Check the system Settings → Notifications → Life Assist, and the
in-app Reminders screen's schedule. Reminders are local
notifications; Focus modes and Scheduled Summary can delay them.
